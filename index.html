Overview

An introduction to the theory and practice of machine learning. Students will implement and evaluate core ML algorithms from scratch, then apply modern libraries to real datasets. The course balances mathematical foundations with hands-on coding.

By the end of the course you should be able to formulate ML problems, select appropriate models, train and tune them, and critically evaluate their performance.
